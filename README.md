SANDLINE: STORM PROTOCOL — a three.js tactical FPS where the desert itself fights back. Classic 5v5 plant/defuse (VIPERS vs JACKALS, buy phase, economy, smoke/flash, four weapons) on the SANDLINE YARD map, twisted by two systems no other entry has: **rolling sandstorms** that sweep the map twice a round, closing the fog, dimming the sun, howling wind audio, and shrinking bot sight range so every mid-round fight turns close-quarters; and the **FALCON pulse drone** (press T, one charge a round, a second buyable for $400) that climbs to 9 m and pings every living enemy within 48 m onto your screen and a live tactical minimap. Play it in any desktop browser with WebGL 2 — click to lock the mouse, WASD to move, plant the bomb at A or B, or defuse it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser with WebGL 2.
